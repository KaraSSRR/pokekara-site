VITALE: Football Career

Live the career. Not just the matches.

A mobile football career simulator and narrative RPG. Grow from prospect to legend in a persistent football world, where transfers, media, money and the national team shape a career that lasts for years, not just a single session.

One career.
An entire football life.

VITALE isn’t about the next match. It’s about the whole arc of a footballer’s life, from the first professional contract to the last walk off the pitch.

You train and you play, but you also negotiate, move clubs, face the press, earn and spend, represent your country and decide what kind of footballer, and person, you become. The football world keeps evolving around you the whole time.

The mobile experience

A football life that fits in one hand.

VITALE is designed for portrait play and one thumb from the very first screen. It isn’t a console game squeezed onto a phone. Six sections hold the whole career.

⌂Home
Your season at a glance: the next fixture, your condition and the signals from the world around you.
▲Training
Plan your week, choose what to develop and manage your workload against the risk of injury.
€Business
Salary, savings, sponsorships, lifestyle and investments. Money matters, off the pitch.
◎Media
Headlines, fan reactions and press conferences, where your answers shape your reputation.
★Career
Your profile, stats, milestones, contract and transfer status: the record of who you are becoming.
◍World
Leagues, cups, international football and the transfer market, all moving with or without you.

Built differently.

Simulation first. 3D where it matters.

What we don’t build

A giant, empty open-world 3D city to wander through.

It costs a fortune and adds little to a footballer’s story. We made a deliberate choice to spend those resources where players actually feel them.

  1. 01

    Simulation depth

    Matches, development, fatigue, form and the market, simulated rather than scripted.

  2. 02

    Persistent world state

    Clubs, competitions and footballers evolve season after season.

  3. 03

    Meaningful mobile UX

    Decisions that are fast to make and matter for years.

  4. 04

    Quality characters

    Footballers who look and feel like people, not placeholders.

  5. 05

    High-impact 3D career moments

    3D reserved for the moments a career is remembered by.

Technology & production

Simulation drives it. Unity shows it. Blender gives it a face.

VITALE is built as a simulation platform with a real 3D production pipeline behind it, not as a collection of screens.

  1. 01 · Simulation systems

    Decides what happens

    Headless systems run the football world: who plays, how matches unfold, how players develop and age, and where careers lead.

    • MatchEngine
    • WorldEngine
    • Career systems
    • Competitions
    • Transfers
    • Data pipeline

  2. 02 · Unity mobile client

    Shows it on your phone

    The portrait mobile app that turns simulation state into screens, decisions and selected 3D scenes.

    • Portrait UI
    • Career screens
    • Presentation
    • 3D scenes

  3. 03 · Blender production

    Gives it a face

    Characters and high-impact career moments are created in Blender and prepared as mobile-ready assets for the Unity client.

    • Characters
    • Event scenes
    • Mobile-ready assets

Underneath it all: automated QA, regression testing and deterministic simulation checks. Run the same match with the same inputs and you get the same result, which is what makes a deep simulation testable and balanceable over time.

For investors & partners

Why VITALE can become more than a single game

Football is one of the world’s largest entertainment categories. VITALE enters it from an angle the big match-control franchises don’t own: a deep career simulation, designed for mobile.

Thesis Why it matters What exists today
01

Deep retention potential

A career unfolds over many in-game seasons in a persistent world. That gives players structural reasons to come back: the next contract, the next tournament, the next chapter.

A persistent WorldEngine and player-lifecycle systems in active engineering. Retention is unproven before release and will be measured, not assumed.

02

Expandable content platform

New leagues, competitions, national teams, events, media and lifestyle systems, and career pathways can extend the game over time, and support future products on the same platform.

Data-driven simulation, with the engine separated from content and presentation.

03

AI-assisted engineering leverage

A small team can build, test and review systems that would normally need a much larger engineering organisation, without giving up human product control.

Claude embedded in architecture, implementation, testing and review, with every change accepted by a person. See how.

04

Mobile-first commercial strategy

Built for the platforms where football fans already spend their time, with UX designed for phones rather than ported to them.

Portrait, one-thumb client in development. Commercial release planned for the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Transparency. VITALE is pre-release. We don’t publish downloads, revenue, retention or ratings until they exist and can be verified. For partnership and investment conversations, email anatolii@pokekara.online.

The studio

About Pokekara Studio

Pokekara Studio is an independent game development initiative based in Ukraine, led by developer Anatolii Ushakov.

Our first title, VITALE: Football Career, brings together deterministic football simulation, player development, club decisions and a persistent world designed for a mobile career RPG.

We are founder-led and bootstrapped, in active pre-release development. The studio is not currently a separately incorporated company. We do not claim investment, sales or an established user base.

Claude Code plays a central role in our game-system design, software architecture, C# implementation, testing, review and creative production workflows. Anatolii directs the project and approves the results.
